# Switch recordings

The recordings in this folder come from **kbsim** by Thomas Lai
(https://github.com/tplai/kbsim, commit ba103f3b0afa9dab80447aa2e7e2ed80b6bd80e4,
`src/assets/audio`), used under the MIT License below. They are unmodified;
Clacky trims leading silence and normalises levels when it loads them.

kbsim does not say who made the original recordings.
